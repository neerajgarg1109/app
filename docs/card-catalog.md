## Data and interpretation

- `backend/cards_catalog.json` holds issuer facts, official product links, citations and the verification date. Descriptions are original summaries; no issuer logos or artwork are included.
- Fees below exclude GST unless nil. First-year/joining fees can differ from renewal fees. A spend waiver is conditional, not lifetime-free. Acquisition offers must be confirmed at application.
- Reward points, airline miles, myCash and Amazon Pay balance are different currencies. Accelerated rates apply only to eligible categories, often with caps. Read each card’s highlights and linked issuer terms.
- `bestFor`, `feeType`, `minSpend` and `creditReq` are editorial quiz hints. They do not assert issuer credit-score cutoffs or guarantee approval. Secured cards explicitly require fixed deposits.
- `rating` is a neutral 4.0 baseline for all cards, retained for compatibility and without a tie-breaking bias. It is not an issuer score or customer review average. Confidence is a quiz-fit estimate, not an approval probability.
- Where a source does not reliably establish an issuing-network variant, `network` says “Issuer-selected variant” rather than guessing.
- HDFC’s website rejected direct automated access. Tata Neu Infinity and Plus were verified using the official Tata Neu co-brand partner site and its published fee, benefit and FAQ content. The FAQ provides their exact HDFC product links. Other unverified HDFC candidates remain excluded. NeuCoins are a restricted redemption currency, not cash; extra NeuPass rewards are separate. Standard joining fees are retained alongside the current conditional promotional waiver.

SBI’s current product pages load their details from official `/json/templatedata/…` endpoints; those URLs are cited as well as the product pages.

Startup upserts all 192 records and marks only the eight known demo IDs inactive. It does not delete demo documents, leads, results or user-added cards. Current `/api/cards` excludes archived demos; new recommendations also exclude paused applications and products whose invitation or existing-card eligibility is not established by the quiz. Direct card lookups remain available for history. New results store scored card snapshots so later issuer updates do not silently change shared pages or email recommendations. Legacy results resolve their saved card IDs, including archived demos.
