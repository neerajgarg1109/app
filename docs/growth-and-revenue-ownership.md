# CardIQ growth and revenue ownership

## What Codex can build and operate in the product

- Maintain crawlable card, issuer, category and editorial pages with unique metadata, internal links and a sitemap.
- Add comparison guides, calculators, FAQs, change histories and structured card data from approved sources.
- Build partner-link versioning, official-link fallback, click attribution and admin reporting.
- Keep affiliate links separate from recommendation ranking and show the customer a clear disclosure.
- Add first-party funnel measurement from organic landing through recommendation and issuer handoff.
- Add automated tests for content completeness, stale pages, broken internal links and unsafe claims.
- Add a supplied Search Console verification file or meta tag and analytics identifier.
- Build partner reconciliation imports once a partner supplies its reporting format.

## What the business owner must do

- Buy and control the production domain and DNS account.
- Decide the final brand/domain and connect it to the selected hosting platform.
- Create and own Google Search Console, analytics and business email accounts.
- Apply to banks, card issuers or affiliate networks and sign commercial agreements.
- Obtain the approved partner URLs, partner IDs, campaign IDs, payout rules and reporting files.
- Confirm which application channels may legally and contractually be promoted.
- Arrange legal review of the privacy policy, terms, affiliate disclosure, consent language and Indian regulatory obligations.
- Provide business identity, contact, grievance and editorial-responsibility details for public trust pages.
- Reconcile partner-reported applications, approvals, reversals and payouts against CardIQ clicks.

## Current commercial baseline — 17 October 2026

- The catalogue contains 254 sourced cards and 215 currently recommendable products.
- All 254 stored application URLs are official issuer or official partner source URLs.
- No catalogue link is inherently revenue-generating until a contracted partner URL is configured in the admin portal.
- CardIQ records application clicks, but confirmed applications, approvals and paid commission require partner reporting.

## Activation sequence

1. Owner chooses and buys the domain.
2. Codex configures the domain, canonical origin, sitemap and supplied Search Console verification.
3. Owner signs the first partner agreement and supplies its link/campaign details.
4. Codex enters or imports the partner configuration in **Testing** status and validates the destination.
5. Owner confirms the commercial and compliance details.
6. Codex changes the link to **Live**; customers then see the partner destination and affiliate disclosure.
7. CardIQ measures clicks by card, partner and campaign while retaining the official URL as fallback.
8. Owner supplies the first reconciliation report; Codex builds the repeatable application/approval/revenue import.

## Weekly operating scorecard

- Search impressions, indexed pages, organic clicks and search click-through rate.
- Organic sessions by card, issuer, category and editorial page.
- Quiz start/completion, comparison usage and application handoff rate.
- Partner-link coverage and monetised click rate.
- Partner-confirmed applications, approvals, reversals, commission and revenue per organic visitor.
- Outdated-card reports, broken destinations and cards awaiting re-verification.
